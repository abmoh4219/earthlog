# Responsible use

Earthlog replays real events in which real people were hurt. These rules shape what we build and what we accept in contributions.

1. **Truth over drama.**
   - Every narrated sentence cites recorded data, and the validator enforces it.
   - No invented facts, no speculation presented as fact, no "bombshell" framing.
   - Estimates and interpolations are labelled.
2. **Measure what the data measures.**
   - Internet signals are connectivity, not people.
   - Fire detections are thermal anomalies, not confirmed damage.
   - Casualty figures appear only when cited from an authoritative source, and never as a spectacle.
3. **No tracking of individuals.** No private persons, private aircraft (honour ADS-B privacy programmes when flights arrive), homes or personal devices. Infrastructure, natural phenomena and aggregate signals only.
4. **No impersonation.** Earthlog never mimics a news outlet's branding or voice. AI-written narration is labelled as such.
5. **Respect the sources.** Every dataset is credited, its licence is honoured, and its limits are stated.
6. **War, conflict and politics: sensors only.**
   - Narration states only what instruments recorded: connectivity fell to 4% of normal; thermal anomalies were detected at these coordinates.
   - **Cause, blame, actors and casualties** appear only as a cited `report` evidence item from a reputable source (for example the Internet Society, Access Now, UN agencies or a major wire service), quoted and attributed: "Reuters reported…". **Never in Earthlog's own voice.**
   - **No conflict replay of anything less than 30 days old.** We never show anything with tactical value.
   - Neutral language: no "enemy", no loaded terms, the same rules for every side.
   - No election results or campaign content.
7. **Sports and culture are joyful, but still true.** "Argentina's internet traffic fell 28% during the final" needs a baseline comparison (the same hours on a normal day) before we claim the match caused it.
8. **Contributions follow the same rules.** Event pull requests that break these rules are declined, however good they look.

These rules also follow Anthropic's Usage Policy, which applies wherever Claude writes narration.
